# Reference: NotebookLM (Audio + Video Overviews)

Sources: Google NotebookLM help page and Google blog (checked Oct 2026). Verify by using it yourself.

---

## What it is
- **Audio Overview:** two AI hosts discuss your sources like a podcast.
- **Video Overview:** a narrated video built from your sources. Formats: Explainer, Brief/Short (~60 sec), Cinematic.
- **Visual styles:** Classic, **Whiteboard**, Watercolor, Anime, Papercraft, Retro Print, Heritage, Kawaii.
- **Hindi supported** (Explainer + Short). Cinematic is English only.

## Warning: this is a direct competitor
NotebookLM's Whiteboard style already offers "upload material -> whiteboard-style video". Brofessor's difference must be unmistakable.

## Their limitations (our gap)
| NotebookLM | Brofessor |
|---|---|
| Visuals are **AI-generated images** (Nano Banana). Google itself warns they may contain inaccuracies | Everything is **drawn stroke by stroke** from data or traced contours, so labels and structure are exact |
| Pre-rendered video; generation can take **30+ minutes** | **Live**: the first step starts within seconds |
| Play / pause / seek only. **No questions mid-video** | Pause, ask a doubt, get the answer **on the same board** |
| Hindi, but not **code-mixed Hinglish** | Hinglish is first-class |
| Overview / summary style | **Step-by-step teaching and solving** (Solvely-style) |

Solvely's step-by-step content + NotebookLM's two-host conversational style + Brofessor's live board.
